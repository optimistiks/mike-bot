# One Conversation per open

A Chat used to keep a single Conversation row that closed and reopened, so Sentry grouped every open as one conversation id. A Conversation is now one bot talk in a Chat: minted closed, opened once by Wake, closed by the last Stop, never opened again. Postgres unique indexes keep at most one open Conversation (`closed_at` null), at most one unopened Conversation (`opened_at` null), and at most one of those two states at a time (`opened_at` null or `closed_at` null). The third index is what stops a concurrent Wake from inserting a new unopened row beside an open one; the loser joins the open Conversation. Turns stay on the Conversation. The next open copies at most 100 newest Turns from the previous finished Conversation when it is short of that window. Finished Conversations keep all their Turns.
